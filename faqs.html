Faqs

With fourteen different events to participate in, find the events that you're best at and be trained you at your strengths!
Track and field is a unique sport that has a relaxed atmosphere with intense moments. Develop your athleticism, build mental strength and compete individually towards team goals!

In a club sport? Not a problem! You can be on the track and field team at the same time you are in other activities. Sign up through the schools athletic registration page.
Any questions can be sent to Andrew Lange | langea@oasd.org
You can compete in your club sports and school extra curriculars during the track and field season! We will work with your schedule to make sure you only practice and compete when it is safe to.
Sports, extra curriculars and off-season conditioning. Track and field team success is tied to the other school activities successes! If track and field is your only sport and you are not sure what others to try, ask us any questions you have!
Visit the schools athletic registration page and sign up for track and field!
If you have any questions, please email head coach Andrew Lange | langea@oasd.org
Whether it's volunteering your time with the booster club, working home meets or helping with the middle school programs, it all goes a long way towards the teams success! We do welcome financial contributions if you are interested in supporting that way as well!
Andrew Lange | langea@oasd.org
